By Harrison Thrift By Harrison Thrift Something that has always attracted my attention in the vast world of literature is the one in which the ancient Greeks crafted long ago. It’s what inspired me to become a teacher in my high school years, and has now led me to create the novel of which I am most proud: The Misadventures of a Grecian. This story–concocted with much love, care, and a healthy dose of my own humor–is a story that was, quite frankly, a lot of fun to create.
