An introduction to Martin Duffy’s new book, DAD By Martin Duffy I have been a writer since I was about ten years old. Could be earlier. I believe I inherited that from my father, who was apparently known for sitting at the bar in a pub, telling a great story, then walking out before the ending, leaving the other men in the pub gasping to know how the story ended.
