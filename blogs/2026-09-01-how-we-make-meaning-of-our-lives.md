By Rivka Gevurtz By Rivka Gevurtz Myths, Fairy Tales, Scriptures, Memoirs – they are all about how we make meaning of our lives. We humans are distinct from our animal relations in this one way – other beings have pain, joy, rage, connection – but only we mythologize our lives and try to find common threads with the lived experiences of others. As I say in Love, By Any Name , a life’s story is a framing of one’s own myths (or those held about us by others) to make meaning of life’s liminal moments, both joyful and painful.
