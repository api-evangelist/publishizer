By Ana Iñigo Olea By Ana Iñigo Olea “I felt nothing up there.” Those were the words a friend shared with me once. He was referring to the podium where he had received a long-pursued award in a competition that would catapult his career to new heights. These words stuck with me, making me wonder about the relationship between achieving our goals and the satisfaction we expect them to bring.
