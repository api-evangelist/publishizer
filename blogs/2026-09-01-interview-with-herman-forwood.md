Author of Secrets and Murders: Walls Dividing Truth Your first three books span three very different genres, from the thriller mystery Echoes of the Ancients, to the historical fiction Twilight of Democracy, and now the dystopian murder mystery Secrets and Murders. When you start a new book, does the genre come first and shape the story, or does an idea come to you first, leading you to discover the right genre for it? I really don’t think what genre I want to write in, instead I focus on what story would be interesting.
