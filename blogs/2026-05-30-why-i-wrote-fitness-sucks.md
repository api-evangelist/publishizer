By Anthony Moreno When most people hear the word “fitness,” they picture a very specific type of environment: loud music, neon lights, beautiful people grunting aggressively while lifting weights, and a twenty-something trainer yelling motivational phrases like a caffeinated life coach. And if you’re like most people, you probably don’t fully see yourself there. In fact, you’d probably feel judged, overwhelmed, or like you don’t belong.
