An interview with Scarlett Yang Scarlett Yang is a London-based Chinese illustrator and book cover designer. She blends Eastern and Western influences to explore identity, feminism, and human relationships. Her surreal, symbolic style embraces ambiguity and duality, inviting reflection on beauty and violence, clarity and obscurity.
